Common questions

My daily reminder doesn't arrive

Open Your peace (the settings button) and check the reminder time. Then check that notifications for Sand Peace are allowed: on iPhone in Settings > Notifications > Sand Peace, on Android in Settings > Apps > Sand Peace > Notifications. Focus modes and battery savers can also hold notifications back.

Saving a video or image to Photos doesn't work

Sand Peace asks once for permission to add to your photo library. If you said no, allow it on iPhone in Settings > Sand Peace > Photos (choose "Add Photos Only"). Sand Peace can only add photos; it never reads your library.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your days of peace and settings are saved on your device only.

I got a new phone and my days of peace are gone

Because nothing is stored with us, days of peace stay on the device where you earned them and can't be moved.

How do I turn off anonymous statistics?

Open Your peace and set Anonymous statistics to Off. The app stops sending straight away and deletes its random ID. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

  • Everything on your device: delete the app (or clear the site's data in your browser).
  • Email news: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us.
  • Focus board (web): tap "Remove my name" on the board, or write to us.

Something looks wrong or the app won't start

Close the app fully and open it again. If it still happens, write to us with your phone model and system version. A screenshot helps a lot.
