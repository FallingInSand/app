Questions

What is Sand Peace?
Sand Peace is a one-minute daily ritual in sand, made by FallingInSand. Each day there is one quiet question. You write your answer in a tray of fine sand, then watch a slow arm smooth the sand until your words are gone and the tray is calm again.
How does the ritual work?
Open the app and read today's question. Write your answer in the sand with your finger or mouse. Then let go: the arm passes over the tray and smooths it away, grain by grain. That is the whole ritual. One minute, once a day.
Is Sand Peace a digital zen garden?
It is a digital sand tray in the spirit of a zen garden. The sand moves grain by grain, and a slow arm smooths it the way the arm of the physical Sand Peace tray does. You can also simply draw in it.
Is Sand Peace free?
Yes. Sand Peace is free, with no ads and no account. Scoop opens after seven days of peace and colour sand after thirty.
Where can I use Sand Peace?
In any modern web browser at app.fallinginsand.com, on a phone, tablet or computer. Apps for Android and iPhone are coming to Google Play and the App Store.
What happens to what I write?
It stays on your device. FallingInSand does not receive what you write in the sand. Your days of peace and settings are saved only on your device. Anonymous statistics help improve the app, and you can turn them off in Your peace. See the privacy page for details.
What if I miss a day?
Nothing resets. Days of peace only ever add up, and new backgrounds (Dawn, Sea glass, Gold) open as they do.
Can I keep or share my ritual?
You can record your ritual as a short video and share it, or keep it for yourself. You can also send a message written in sand as a link.
Is Sand Peace a meditation or health app?
No. It is a simple daily ritual for a calm minute and a way to let go of a thought. It is not a meditation course and it is not a health app.
Is there a real Sand Peace tray?
Yes. Sand Peace started as a physical automatic zen garden for your desk, sold at fallinginsand.com. The app brings the same ritual to your phone.
Who makes Sand Peace?
FallingInSand, the sand-art studio behind work millions have watched online. FallingInSand is a brand of FallingIn Media Corp. in Markham, Ontario, Canada.
