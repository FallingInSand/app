Privacy

Sand Peace by FallingInSand · Last updated October 11, 2026

  • No account and no ads.
  • Your days of peace and your settings are saved only on your device.
  • We do not receive what you write in the sand.
  • We do not sell your information or track you across other apps and websites.

This policy covers the Sand Peace app for Android and iOS and the Sand Peace web version at app.fallinginsand.com. Sand Peace is made by FallingIn Media Corp. ("FallingInSand", "we").

What stays on your device

Sand Peace keeps a small amount of information in your device's local app storage so it can work. None of it is sent to us.

The words you write are drawn into the sand on your screen. They are not saved to a server and we never see them.

When the app connects to the internet

The Sand Peace app works fully offline. Its fonts and artwork are built in, so once it is open it does not contact us or anyone else on its own. It only goes online when you tap one of these:

  • Links to our shop. Some buttons open fallinginsand.com, where we sell the physical Sand Peace tray. These links include a tag showing which button you tapped (for example "footer"), so we can see which ones are useful. Our shop runs on Shopify and has its own privacy policy, shown on fallinginsand.com.
  • Calendar reminder (web only). If you add the daily reminder on the web, it opens Google Calendar with the event filled in. What happens next is between you and Google.

If you use Sand Peace in a browser instead, the page is downloaded from app.fallinginsand.com, and our web host sees your IP address while serving it, as with any website. We do not use it to identify you.

What you choose to share

You can record a short clip of your tray, save an image, or share a "message in the sand" link. Sharing always goes through your device's share sheet, to an app and people you choose. A message link holds your drawing inside the link itself, so whoever opens it can see it. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Permissions

  • Notifications, if you allow them, for one daily reminder at the time you choose. The reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Add to Photos (iOS), to save a clip or image when you ask. Sand Peace can add to your library but cannot read it.
  • Vibration (Android) and haptics (iOS), for a gentle tap when you finish a ritual.

Statistics

This version of Sand Peace sends no statistics. If we start collecting anonymous usage statistics, such as how many people complete a ritual each day, we will update this page and the store privacy details first. Those statistics will not include your name, email, contacts, location or what you write, and they will not be used for advertising.

Children

Sand Peace is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Turn off reminders in the app, or in your device's notification settings.
  • Delete everything Sand Peace has saved by uninstalling the app, or on the web by clearing site data for app.fallinginsand.com.
  • Because we do not hold your information on our servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete. If you have a question, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your device, we will update it before that version of the app is released.
