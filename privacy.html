Privacy

Sand Peace by FallingInSand · Last updated October 14, 2026

  • No account and no ads.
  • Your days of peace and your settings are saved only on your device.
  • We do not receive what you write in the sand.
  • We do not sell your information or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Anonymous statistics help us improve the app. You can turn them off in Your peace.
  • If you choose to sign up for email news, your email address joins our shop's mailing list. You can unsubscribe in any email.

This policy covers the Sand Peace app for Android and iOS and the Sand Peace web version at app.fallinginsand.com. Sand Peace is made by FallingIn Media Corp. ("FallingInSand", "we").

What stays on your device

Sand Peace keeps a small amount of information in your device's local app storage so it can work. Apart from the anonymous statistics described below, none of it is sent to us.

The words you write are drawn into the sand on your screen. They are not saved to a server and we never see them.

When the app connects to the internet

The Sand Peace app works fully offline. Its fonts and artwork are built in. Once it is open, the only thing it sends on its own is anonymous statistics (below), which you can turn off. Otherwise it only goes online when you tap one of these:

  • Links to our shop. Some buttons open fallinginsand.com, where we sell the physical Sand Peace tray. These links include a tag showing which button you tapped (for example "footer"), so we can see which ones are useful. Our shop runs on Shopify and has its own privacy policy, shown on fallinginsand.com.
  • Calendar reminder (web only). If you add the daily reminder on the web, it opens Google Calendar with the event filled in. What happens next is between you and Google.

If you use Sand Peace in a browser instead, the page is downloaded from app.fallinginsand.com, and our web host sees your IP address while serving it, as with any website. We do not use it to identify you.

What you choose to share

You can record a short clip of your tray, save an image, share a "message in the sand" link, or share how long a focus session lasted. Sharing always goes through your device's share sheet, to an app and people you choose. A message link holds your drawing inside the link itself, so whoever opens it can see it. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Email news (optional)

In Your peace, and once after your second day of peace, Sand Peace offers email news: new sand, new patterns and the occasional note from the FallingInSand studio, about twice a month. If you sign up:

  • Your email address is sent to our server and passed straight to our shop on Shopify, which keeps our mailing list. Our server doesn't keep it.
  • Shopify records that you agreed and when, and tags the address with where you signed up (for example "sand-peace-app"), so we know the signup came from the app.
  • We use it only to send you these emails. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else. Shopify's own privacy policy applies to the list.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us at the address below to remove you.
  • Nothing in the app depends on signing up.

Focus board (web version only)

On app.fallinginsand.com you can choose to add a focus session to a public board of focus minutes from the last 24 hours. The Android and iOS apps don't have the board.

  • Timing. So that minutes on the board are real, the web version tells our server when a focus session starts and stops, with a random board key made by your browser. If nobody adds the session to the board, it is deleted after a day. If you have turned off anonymous statistics, this only happens once you have joined the board yourself.
  • What becomes public if you join. The name you type, the country you pick (optional; we never detect it), and your focus minutes. Anyone visiting the board can see them. Don't use your full name if you'd rather not be found.
  • How long. Board entries are deleted after 30 days. "Remove my name" on the board deletes them straight away.
  • Reports. Anyone can report a name. A name reported by three people is hidden until we review it, and we remove names that are unkind or not right for the board.
  • We don't store your IP address for the board. To stop abuse, our service provider (Cloudflare) counts requests per connection for a minute, in memory, without keeping them.

Permissions

  • Notifications, if you allow them, for one daily reminder at the time you choose. The reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Add to Photos (iOS), to save a clip or image when you ask. Sand Peace can add to your library but cannot read it.
  • Vibration (Android) and haptics (iOS), for a gentle tap when you finish a ritual.

Anonymous statistics

To learn whether Sand Peace helps people come back to their daily minute, the app sends a short note when something happens in it, such as "app opened", "ritual done" or "reminder set". Each note contains:

  • what happened, and a few plain details about it (for example your total days of peace, the hour you chose for a reminder, or how many minutes a focus session lasted)
  • the date on your device (the day only, not the time)
  • the random ID the app created, so we can count returning visits without knowing who you are
  • whether you use the web, Android or iOS version, the app version, which of two headline wordings you saw, and which link brought you (for example "reminder")

Notes never include your name, email, contacts, location, photos or what you write in the sand, and we do not store your IP address. We use them only to understand and improve Sand Peace: never for advertising, never to track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share them.

The notes are received by a small service we run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare handles the connection, which includes your IP address, to deliver it to us, and is bound by its own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). We keep notes for up to 13 months, then delete them.

Turning it off: open Your peace (the settings button) and set Anonymous statistics to Off. The app stops sending notes straight away and deletes its random ID. If you turn it back on later, it starts with a new ID. Because notes are anonymous, we cannot find or delete the ones already sent from a particular device.

Children

Sand Peace is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are under 13, please don't add your name to the focus board.

Your choices

  • Turn off reminders in the app, or in your device's notification settings.
  • Turn off anonymous statistics in Your peace.
  • Take your name off the focus board with "Remove my name" on the board, or by contacting us.
  • Unsubscribe from email news with the link in any email, or by contacting us.
  • Delete everything Sand Peace has saved by uninstalling the app, or on the web by clearing site data for app.fallinginsand.com.
  • Apart from your email address if you sign up for news, and what you add to the focus board, the only information we hold is the anonymous statistics above, which we cannot link to you. If you have a question, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your device, we will update it before that version of the app is released.
